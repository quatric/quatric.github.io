# AI Usage Policy

I understand that there are many people who love AI, and many who hate AI. That's why I wrote this policy - for transparency.

All of my projects are AI-driven, and today I almost always use Claude to write them. Most of the work I do would be very hard to humanly code, meaning that my projects wouldn't exist without it. AI is being used for my projects in these ways:

- Writing code, in various languages, usually Python, C, or C++
- Reverse engineering compiled code
- The planning, designing, and the UI/UX of the project

The goal is to provide fun and/or useful applications, primarily for Nintendo devices, but occasionally others. I have programming skills, which does help steer Claude in the right direction.

I changed my name, and focus, to start a new era with my work in the homebrew community in the wake of AI.

Here's what I pledge to do, for my projects.

- Anything I release will be free to use.
- I strive to innovate and make unique projects that I wanted to personally see be made in the community. Despite AI being used to write code, I strive to work hard to facilitate my projects.
- I will not use AI to create imagery, videos, or games, other than things like making simple logos, UI/UX designs, or otherwise.
- I strive to do basic testing of my applications to ensure that things work before making an initial release. If bugs show up, I strive to fix them in the next release.
- I enjoy, and strive to, collaborate with other users to contribute ideas or code with.

Even though AI is used to write most of the code, many people who develop projects with AI are human and are controlling it. They deserve respect.

This usage policy was written without the use of AI.
